For beginners

A Video Editor for People Who Get Lost in Final Cut Pro or Premiere

There's a specific kind of frustrating experience a lot of beginners have: you buy Final Cut Pro or Premiere Pro because everyone says it's "the" tool, you open it, and you're immediately faced with a dozen panels, timelines, inspectors, and settings you don't understand — for a video you just wanted to trim and post.

That's not a personal failing. Those tools are genuinely built for people who already know how to edit professionally. If you don't, the learning curve isn't small — it's a real barrier between you and just posting your video.

The usual advice doesn't actually solve this

Most "beginner-friendly" alternatives (Premiere Elements, Filmora, iMovie) are still, fundamentally, manual editors — you're still doing every cut, every trim, every decision by hand. They're simpler, but they don't remove the actual work. You still need to know what to do, just with fewer buttons.

What's actually different about a good fit here

The real fix for "I don't know how to edit and don't want to learn" isn't a simpler manual editor — it's a tool that makes the editing decisions for you, automatically, and just lets you review and adjust the result.

That's the specific thing I'm building with Cutaway:

  • Automatic dead-air removal — it finds and cuts the boring parts itself
  • Automatic highlight detection — it finds the best moments in your footage on its own
  • One-click templates that combine those into a finished style
  • Manual trim/split/cut tools too, for when you do want direct control — but you're never required to use them

Built by someone who had this exact problem

I'm not a professional editor. I bought Final Cut Pro, got lost in it almost immediately, and decided to build something myself instead — specifically for people who want their raw footage turned into something postable without first becoming a video editor.

It's a personal project, actively being built in public, with no fixed deadline — just seeing how far I can take it.

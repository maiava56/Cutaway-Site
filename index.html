A personal project

I got lost in Final Cut Pro.
So I'm building my own editor.

Not because Final Cut is bad software — it's genuinely good. But it's built for people who already know how to edit. I didn't. I just had a phone full of raw footage and no idea how to turn any of it into something worth posting.

See how it works Follow the build

The problem

This isn't Cutaway vs. Final Cut Pro.

I'm not trying to build a smaller Premiere, or a free Final Cut. That's not a fight worth having — those tools have years of professional tooling behind them.

What I'm actually building is for a different moment: the 30-60 minutes of raw footage that would otherwise just sit there, unedited, forever — because opening a professional editor for it feels like more work than it's worth.

What it does right now

Built to make the editing decisions for you.

Cuts the dead air

Finds and removes silence and pauses automatically — no more scrubbing to find where to cut.

Finds the best moments

Motion and audio scoring picks out what's actually worth keeping, on its own.

Random sampler

Different clips picked every time you run it — never the same result twice.

One-click templates

Fast-paced highlights, a chill montage, or just a clean trim — pick a style, it builds it.

Text and captions

Add your own text directly on the video, drag it into place — including non-Latin scripts.

Real 4K export

True upscaled 4K output, not just a resized file.

Trim, split, and cut

Drag to trim, split at a point, remove a piece — with full undo.

Mix multiple videos

Pull pieces from more than one source video into a single timeline and export the mix.

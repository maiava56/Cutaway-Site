Free vs Pro

The line is simple: who's making the decision.

If you're doing the editing yourself, it's free. If the AI is doing it for you, that's Pro. Here's exactly where that line sits, and why.

Free, always

Manual editing

  • Trim, split, and cut — with full undo
  • Text and captions, including non-Latin scripts
  • VidBash — mix clips from multiple videos
  • Color filters (Vibrant, B&W, Warm, Cool)
  • Aspect ratio reframing and true 4K export
  • Random sampler
  • Project save and load

Everything here is you making the decisions. Cutaway just gets out of your way.

Pro

AI does the editing

  • Cut dead air — automatically finds and removes it

Everything here is Cutaway making a real judgment call about your specific footage — not a fixed preset, an actual decision. Highlight Reel used to live here too — pulled for now after repeated real-world failures on long, high-res footage. See the changelog for the full story; it's paused for a rebuild, not abandoned.

Why isn't all of it free?

The honest answer

The manual tools — trim, text, mixing, filters — are things basically any video editor can do, free, forever. That's the generous baseline, and it's not going away.

The AI side is different. Getting a computer to actually look at raw footage and correctly judge "this part is dead air" or "this moment is worth keeping" is genuinely hard, ongoing work — not a one-time feature you build and forget. It's the part that's taken the most iteration, the most debugging, the most real engineering effort of anything in this whole project.

If that part were free too, there'd be no way to keep actually building it — no time to keep improving the scoring, fixing edge cases, or adding the next AI feature. The Pro tier isn't a paywall on the app. It's what makes it possible for the app to keep getting better instead of being a personal project that quietly stops one day.

So the free tier isn't a stripped-down demo — it's a genuinely complete manual editor on its own. Pro is there for the part that actually costs something to keep building: the AI figuring it out for you.

No launch date. No deadline.

Just seeing how far I can take this — built in public, one part at a time.

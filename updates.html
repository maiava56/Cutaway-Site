Changelog

Everything shipped, big and small.

The main series covers the big features. This page is everything else — fixes, polish, and smaller additions that don't need their own Part.

Site

Added the Series and Updates pages

Split the growing video collection into its own page so the homepage stays short as more Parts get posted. This page is the other half of that same cleanup.

Fix

VidBash: fixed an audio glitch between mismatched source clips

Mixing clips from two different source files could produce a glitch right at the transition when their audio formats didn't match. Rebuilt the join step to normalize audio before combining, and added a live preview player so trimming a piece is no longer done blind.

Feature

VidBash — mix clips from multiple videos

Import more than one source video, pull pieces from each into a single sequence, and export the mix — including different resolutions and frame rates from different sources, normalized automatically.

Feature

Real 4K upscale export

What used to be a locked placeholder is now genuine orientation-aware 4K upscaling — vertical, square, or landscape.

Feature

Project save and load

Trims, cuts, and text overlays now persist — close the app and pick up exactly where you left off instead of starting over.

Feature

Score transparency for Highlight Reel

Every clip Highlight Reel picks now shows why — a plain-language reason plus a motion/audio breakdown, instead of an unexplained pick.

Feature

Trim, Split & Cut redesign

Rebuilt trim to be QuickTime-style and draggable, added a dedicated Split & Cut mode with click-to-position and full undo, plus precise timecode entry for frame-accurate work. Fixed several real bugs along the way: the timeline going thin when text was added, cuts becoming invisible after exiting edit mode, and a handful of broken filmstrip thumbnails.

Feature

Text and captions

Add your own text directly on the video — fonts, bold/italic, color, drag-to-position — including non-Latin scripts like Korean.

Feature

Templates and Random Sampler

One-click editing styles (fast-paced, chill, or just cleaned up), plus a genuinely randomized sampler that pulls a different set of clips every time it runs.

Feature

Highlight Reel

Automatic detection of the most interesting moments in raw footage, using motion and audio energy.

Feature

Cut dead air

The first real feature — automatic detection and removal of silence and dead pauses.

No launch date. No deadline.

Just seeing how far I can take this — built in public, one part at a time.
